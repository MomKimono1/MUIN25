MU CADPDF V25 - SAVE AS & AUTO OPEN (AutoCAD 2021)

PHIEN BAN NAY DUOC SUA TREN V25 RESET MODEL. KHONG DUNG PDF24.

CHI 2 THAY DOI:
1. Sau khi chon cac khung va chon thong so in, AutoCAD hien Save As: chon thu muc va ten file PDF.
2. Khi MU24CORE xuat PDF thanh cong, chuong trinh tu mo file bang ung dung PDF mac dinh cua Windows (Foxit, Edge, Adobe...).

CAI DAT:
1. Mo dung file DWG trong AutoCAD 2021.
2. NETLOAD -> MU24Direct.dll da bien dich va dung tren may ban. KHONG CAN BIEN DICH LAI DLL chi de them Save As / Open.
3. APPLOAD -> MU_CADPDF_V25_SAVE_OPEN.lsp.
4. Go MUIN25SAVE (hoac MUIN25); chon khung + CHON TO, Enter.
5. Bam CHON GIAY / LUU PDF. Chon may in PDF, A3 va monochrome.ctb; OK.
6. Hop thoai Save As xuat hien: chon noi luu, dat ten PDF, bam Save.
7. Neu xuat thanh cong, PDF se duoc mo bang ung dung PDF mac dinh.

AN TOAN:
- Neu huy Save As: KHONG in.
- Neu file PDF da ton tai: yeu cau chon ten moi, KHONG tu ghi de.
- Neu MU24CORE bao loi va khong tao duoc file PDF: KHONG mo file.
- Khong tao PDF trang le, khong copy DWG, khong goi PDF24.
- Dong hop thoai: V25 van xoa khung danh dau va reset danh sach.

GIOI HAN:
- Day la sua doi LISP tren nen V25. No VAN CAN DLL MU24Direct.dll duoc NETLOAD.
- Neu DLL cu co loi rieng (chua xuat thanh cong), thay doi Save As khong sua duoc loi PlotEngine cua DLL.
