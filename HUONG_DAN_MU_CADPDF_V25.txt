MU CADPDF V25 -- RESET + MODEL/LAYOUT

1. Tai va giai nen MU_CADPDF_V25_RESET_MODEL.zip.
2. Trong AutoCAD 2021: NETLOAD MU24Direct.dll (ban da bien dich o V24; khong can bien dich lai neu DLL da nap).
3. APPLOAD MU_CADPDF_V25_RESET_MODEL.lsp tai DUNG tab file DWG dang xu ly.
4. Go MU25CHECK de xem MODE va so khung CAD da nhan.
5. Go MUIN25. Chon + CHON TO, nhap 1 diem ben trong khung. Enter ket thuc chon.
6. Co the LEN / XUONG / XOA / XEM TO. In dung MU24CORE nhu truoc.
7. Bam DONG, hoac dau X tren hop thoai: cac vien do + chu so vang se tu dong xoa,
   danh sach da chon duoc RESET. Mo MUIN25 lan nua se BAT DAU LAI tu 0 to.
8. Ho tro Model tab (TILEMODE=1): chuyen vao tab Model, go MU25CHECK / MUIN25.
   Neu o Layout va muon chon khung trong Paper: go PSPACE truoc khi dung.

THAY DOI V25:
- Reset trang thai khi DONG, X hoac Escape; reset ca khi gap loi.
- Reset khi mo lai, khong nhan ban ve cu.
- Model: do quet ca INSERT Xref/Block va LWPOLYLINE khung RECTANG.
- Chon duy nhat mot file PDF nhieu trang bang AutoCAD .NET MU24CORE.
- Khong dung PDF24 Creator, khong COPY DWG, khong tao PDF trang le.
- Co lenh MU25CHECK kiem tra Model / Paper va so khung nhan dien.

Luu y: khung Model phai la INSERT co bao khung hoac RECTANG/LWPOLYLINE kin
co ti le giay; khung chi gom 4 LINE roi chua duoc quet tu dong.
Code duoc kiem tra tĩnh (dau ngoac, tham chieu lenh); chua thu truc tiep tren AutoCAD 2021.
